# IVA Panel features

## Subscriptions and clients

- Automatic subscriptions, Base64, QR Code, and configuration lists
- Clash/Mihomo, sing-box, Loon, Surge, and Quantumult X
- Client auto-detection and Mixed Auto
- Direct VLESS, Trojan, Shadowsocks, and Clash links
- Per-user URLs with username, secret key, quota, expiration, and status

## Network and routing

- Load Balancing, Failover, Health Check, and Proxy Fallback
- GeoIP, GeoSite, Domestic Bypass, and custom routing rules
- IPv6, NAT64, WARP, WARP+, WoW, and Amnezia WARP
- Clean Cloudflare IP scanner and per-ISP optimization
- WebSocket, gRPC, XHTTP, port spread, and multiple transports
- National Internet, Arvan CDN, and Google Apps Script relays in v3

## DNS and protection

- DoH, encrypted DNS, Local DNS, and Anti-Sanction DNS
- DNS Load Balance, Failover, and caching
- Ad, adult content, malware, phishing, and cryptominer blocking
- QUIC blocking and Fake DNS

## Administration

- Responsive Persian RTL dashboard
- Simple and advanced modes, dark theme, and onboarding tour
- JSON editor, log viewer, and configuration reset
- Traffic charts, user management, and fleet statistics
- Password changes, TOTP, recovery codes, login limits, and sessions
- Full Backup/Restore and KV-to-D1 migration

## Automation

- Telegram management and notifications
- Central management API, fleet announcements, and heartbeat
- `/install` wizard and one-click deployment

See [[Feature Matrix]] for the detailed v1/v2/v3 comparison.

> Network behavior depends on the ISP, current internet conditions, Cloudflare quotas, domains, and user configuration. No route can guarantee continuous access or a specific latency.
