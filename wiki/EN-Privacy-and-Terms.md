# Privacy and Terms

## Owner and contact

The official project owner is **IVA Team**. Official email: `info@ivaworks.site`.

## Data IVA does not collect or store

- Cloudflare API Tokens and account information
- User IP address, panel password, and private configuration
- Installation or activity logs
- Analytics, behavioral advertising, or tracking cookies

Installation requests are sent directly from the user's device to Cloudflare. The documentation website is static and has no application backend. Information voluntarily included in a support request is used only to answer that request.

## Third-party services

Cloudflare, GitHub, Telegram, and the hosting provider operate under their own terms and privacy policies. IVA does not control those independent policies.

## Age requirement

The service is for people aged 18 or older, or users with permission from a legal guardian.

Users must follow applicable law, Cloudflare's terms, and third-party rights. Bulk account creation, quota evasion, unauthorized access, harm, phishing, malware, and rights violations are prohibited.

Users are responsible for protecting their Cloudflare account, tokens, passwords, TOTP, recovery codes, backups, and private URLs. IVA does not guarantee permanent connectivity, a particular latency, or third-party availability.
